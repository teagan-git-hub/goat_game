README — Pixel Animals Pack (16×16) - FREE VERSION (Only 4 animals with 1 color variants)
Overview

A stylized pixel art animals pack designed for 2D games such as RPGs, farming sims, platformers, and cozy games.

The pack includes a variety of animals in multiple color variants, provided on sprite sheets and ready to be sliced in Unity or other engines.

Asset Details

Art style: Pixel art

Base resolution: 16×16 pixels

Format: PNG

Layout: Sprite sheets (sliceable)

Animations: None (static sprites)

Engine compatibility: Unity, Godot, GameMaker, and other 2D engines

Included Animals (FREE VERSION)

Sheep – 1 color variants (Walk, eat, die/sit animations)

Pig – 1 variant (Walk, eat, die/sit animations)

Cow – 1 color variants (Walk (beginning of walk, and loop walk), eat, die/sit animations)

Chicken – 1 color variants (Eat, walk, die animations, sit sprite)

Extra Content

White silhouette versions of all animals
